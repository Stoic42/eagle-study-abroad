## I. Common App 主文书（650 字）

**Prompt 7 — Topic of your choice（推荐）**

### 草稿：The Bicycle Shop Boy Who Built an AI Agent

At 2 AM on a winter night in 2025, I sat in our family bicycle shop, watching the cursor blink on my laptop. Outside, my parents — immigrants from Anhui who had moved to Shanghai to run this modest shop — were asleep in the back room. Inside, I was debugging my first AI Agent, a browser automation tool that would later become Jiancareer, serving 500 job seekers and winning the WaytoAGI Hackathon first prize.

I never set out to build a startup. I just wanted to solve a problem my college-aged friend kept complaining about: filling the same job application form, again and again, for every company. "Why is this still manual?" he asked. I had no answer. So I built one.

But here's the thing — the bicycle shop taught me how to ship.

When I was 16, I noticed our campus had no original postcards. I asked myself: could I make some, sell them, learn how a real business runs? In 24 hours, I designed, printed, and distributed 2,000 postcards across our school. Revenue: 3,500 RMB. It was small. But for the first time, I saw what "shipping" meant: not perfect, not finished, but *out the door*.

I've been shipping ever since.

In 2024, I joined GOGC 2024 — a 1,000-person hackathon — right after finishing my high school entrance exams. I was the youngest on my team of four, surrounded by a college professor, a game design student, and an IT product manager. They didn't look down on me. We built an AI-glasses cultural relic exploration app and won Best Practice Award. That was the first complete cycle I ever experienced: idea → build → demo → pitch.

Since then: WorldRemix XR 2025 (1st place, "Meow Piano" built on Apple Vision Pro + Unity), REBUILD-Z 2025 (1st place AI+Hardware for SilentBridge, a sign language teaching platform), Huawei Developer Competition 2026 (1st place), and AI Agent 2025 (Outstanding Gen-Z Developer). Seven awards. Three production products. Zero apologies for being 17.

In January 2026, I joined Professor Li Zexiang's XbotPark, the legendary hardware startup incubator that helped build DJI. There, I led user research on "lean sugar" type 2 diabetes — 20+ office worker interviews, 5+ physician consultations. Our team won 2nd place for a blood glucose management proposal.

I want to come to [School] because I've proven I can ship — but I haven't yet learned the science of building durable systems. My agents work; my hardware talks; my hackathons win. What I need is the rigorous foundation in computer science theory, the multi-year collaboration with serious engineers, and the patient mentorship to scale beyond hackathons into systems that matter.

The bicycle shop is still open. My parents still work there every day. But I'm building something different now — something that doesn't fit in a 24-hour postcard cycle, but takes years and a community.

I'm ready for that next step.

### UC PIQ 1 — Describe a community contribution（SheNicest）

**草稿**：

SheNicest — China's first hackathon community for "Gen Z + Female + Tech Creator" — didn't exist in 2025. I co-founded it with a teammate because we noticed a gap: at hackathons, female participation rarely exceeded 15%.

We didn't start with money or sponsors. We started with a WeChat group, 12 committed people, and a manifesto: technology creation isn't gendered. Within a year, we delivered 3 large hackathons (Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen), 4 technical workshops, and attracted 2,100+ registrants, 500+ active participants, and 300+ demoable projects. Today, SheNicest's private community has grown to 13,000+ members with 2,000,000+ public reach.

But numbers weren't the point. A 19-year-old computer science student in Shenzhen told me last month that SheNicest was the first time she felt "this is where I belong." That's the contribution I'm proudest of.

As technical lead, I designed the event infrastructure, built the registration system, and mentored 40+ workshop participants. I also negotiated brand sponsorships — uncomfortable work for a 16-year-old — but I learned that building community means building trust with people who don't owe you anything.

**字数**：约 280 字

### UC PIQ 2 — Describe a creative side（NOMO / 自建 PaaS）

**草稿**：

NOMO is my creative side project, and it's not for users — it's for developers like me.

I hate DevOps. After building my 36th GitHub repository, I got tired of the deployment rabbit hole: nginx configs, Docker networks, SSL certificates, Gitea migrations. So in December 2025, I built NOMO — a one-click deployment platform that lets developers push code or upload a ZIP and get a live URL in 60 seconds.

I built it on Coolify (an open-source PaaS), Supabase (database), and a custom Go service (zip-agent) that bridges ZIP uploads to Git repositories. The architecture: User ZIP → NOMO → zip-agent → Gitea → Coolify deploy → live URL. I open-sourced zip-agent on GitHub and tested the system internally.

The creative part wasn't the code — it was the design constraint: zero DevOps knowledge required. I had to think like a developer who'd never seen a YAML file. That mindset shift — from "what can I build" to "what can my user ignore" — is now how I approach every product.

**字数**：约 240 字

### UC PIQ 3 — Describe a challenge（GOGC 2024 — 17 岁与队友合作）

**草稿**：

At GOGC 2024, I was the youngest on my team of four. The others: a college professor, a game design undergraduate, an IT product manager. Me? A high schooler who'd just finished his 中考.

I felt out of place immediately. The professor talked about academic papers. The PM talked about user stories. The design student talked about art direction. I'd never taken a college class.

So I volunteered for the unsexy work: research, documentation, version control. While they sketched, I curated the cultural heritage dataset for our AI-glasses app. While they coded, I wrote the demo script. While they argued about positioning, I asked the user-flow questions.

We won Best Practice Award. But more importantly, I learned a lesson I've carried into every hackathon since: when you're the youngest in the room, the smartest move isn't to prove you belong — it's to make yourself indispensable.

That team didn't need me to be brilliant. They needed me to be reliable. Showing up prepared, finishing what I started, asking the right questions — that's what earned me a seat at the table. It's still what earns me one.

**字数**：约 270 字

### UC PIQ 4 — Why this major（CS — 软硬通吃的兴趣起源）

**草稿**：

I fell in love with computer science at age 10, watching games run on my family's desktop computer. "How is this made?" I wondered. By 11, I was writing Scratch. By 12, Python. By 13, C. I wrote technical blogs on CSDN; one post reached 30,000 views. Readers didn't know the "expert" was in 6th grade.

But software alone wasn't enough. At 14, I built my first six-legged robot. At 15, I made a smart voice device called "小龙蛋" for my school. At 16, I joined XbotPark and studied blood glucose management. At 17, I built SilentBridge — a sign language teaching platform using AI vision + hardware.

What ties these together is a single obsession: how humans interact with systems, and how systems can be made more humane. My hardware projects teach me the physics of the physical world. My AI agents teach me the logic of the digital one. Computer Science is the only discipline that lives in both.

I want to study CS not because I want to be a software engineer — I already am one. I want to study CS to learn the theoretical foundations that make my work durable beyond hackathons: algorithms, systems, theory of computation. I want to stop shipping demos and start building the future.

## III. WAIC 2026 演讲素材

### 演讲主题
**"为什么 AI 时代最稀缺的资源，是愿意认真对待一个疯狂想法的人"**

### 演讲结构（10-15 分钟）

#### 开场：1 分钟 — 真实故事
"再过两天，我将作为少年开发者的代表，登上 2026 世界人工智能大会的舞台，与华为云工程师代表同台对话。主办方眼中的我，是一个'拖着个用废纸箱子手搓的项目介绍就来了'的小孩。但 PPT 上有一句话，我一个字一个字打上去的——"

> **"世界上有一个场域，专门容纳看起来疯狂的想法，而且那里的人不会因为你年轻而笑话你。"**

#### 第一部分：3 分钟 — 自行车专卖店的 AI 启蒙
- 父母从安徽来上海，经营一家自行车专卖店
- 电脑启蒙：家里台机上的单机小游戏
- 自学路径：Scratch → Python → C 语言（"古法编程"时代）
- 6 年级在 CSDN 写博客，最高单帖 3 万+ 浏览
- 读者不知道这个"大神"才 6 年级

#### 第二部分：4 分钟 — 黑客松的真实故事
- GOGC 2024：第一次完整项目经历（最佳实践奖）
- WorldRemix XR 2025：喵喵琴（猫爪虚拟弹琴）→ 总赛道第一
- REBUILD-Z 2025：SilentBridge（手语教育）→ AI+硬件方向一等奖
- 华为开发者大赛 2026：SilentBridge 升级版 → 一等奖
- 队友是大学教师 / 产品经理 / 设计本科生，只有我是高中生
- **核心认知**："当你是房间里最年轻的人，最聪明的动作不是证明你配得上 — 而是让自己不可或缺"

#### 第三部分：3 分钟 — Build in Public 与零到一
- 明信片 24 小时售 2000+ 张 / 收入 ¥3500（第一个商业闭环）
- 简职（AI Agent 网申插件）：500 种子用户 + WaytoAGI 一等奖
- NOMO（开发者一键部署）：消灭 DevOps，让提交代码即可上线
- SheNicest（女性黑客松）：从 12 人微信群到 1.3 万私域 / 200 万公域
- **核心认知**："我追求的不是单一产品，是打通创业全链路的超级个体"

#### 第四部分：2 分钟 — 探索方向与未来
- 具身智能 / AGI / 脑机接口 / 超语言表达系统
- XbotPark（李泽湘体系）：进入顶级硬件创业孵化
- 华东师大 CS 学院 4-bit TD4 计算机：底层硬件基础
- 探索方向：从工具到意义（"不只是做出工具，更是从'会解题'走向'理解人'"）

#### 结尾：1 分钟 — 给同龄人的话
"可以有一些错误的想法，但要和别人不一样，要独特。因为我们还年轻。"

> **"AI 时代最稀缺的资源，不是算力，不是数据，而是愿意认真对待一个疯狂想法的人。如果你身边有一个看起来疯狂的小孩，请不要笑话他。因为那个疯狂的想法，可能是下一个改变世界的起点。"**

### 草稿：当我用自行车专卖店视角看 CS

我来自上海的一个普通家庭——父母从安徽来到上海，经营一家自行车专卖店。他们管我"不松也不紧"，给了我探索世界的时间。

13 岁那年，我在 CSDN 写下了第一篇技术博客。6 年级，最高单帖 3 万浏览。那时候，AI 还不会写代码，每个 bug 我都要自己排查，自己搜索，自己解决。我叫它"古法编程"——那段没有 AI 帮忙、纯靠工程思维的日子，至今仍是我最珍视的训练。

16 岁，我做出了第一个"产品"：校园原创明信片。24 小时内，我从零完成了调研、设计、印刷、分发，售出 2000+ 张，收入约 3500 元。不多，但是**真实的商业闭环**——这是我第一次体会到"做出来"和"卖出去"的距离。

17 岁，我已经联合创立了 SheNicest（女性黑客松厂牌，覆盖 1.3 万私域用户），担任过简职（Jiancareer）的 CTO（AI Agent 网申插件，500 种子用户，WaytoAGI 黑客松一等奖），入选了李泽湘教授的 XbotPark 创业体系（硬件孵化），并以总成绩第一、二等奖等七项大奖完成了一系列黑客松与竞赛。

但这不是一个"成功故事"——**这是我父母用一辆自行车一辆自行车地卖出来的时间。**

他们不懂 AI、不懂 Python、不懂 GitHub。但他们给了我这台电脑、这台显示器、这些时间，以及一个不被笑话的安全场。这是为什么我的 SheNicest 黑客松，从来不接受任何嘲笑；这是为什么我做的 SilentBridge（手语教育平台），从一开始就把"理解人"放在"解决技术问题"之前。

我想去德国学 CS，原因是：**德国是我看到的、把"工程思维"和"人的尺度"结合得最好的地方**。

- 慕尼黑工大的"创业生态系统"
- 亚琛工大的"工业 4.0 实习直通车"
- 柏林工大的"自由探索传统"

这些不只是名校光环——这是欧洲工业 4.0 + 严谨工程文化的最好训练场。我的探索方向（具身智能、AGI、脑机接口）需要的不只是算法，**还需要理解物理世界的工程严谨性**。这是德国 CS 教育能给、而美国顶尖大学给不了的差异化价值。

我不需要最响亮的学校名字。我需要的是一个**让我把"古法编程"的工程思维 + 真实商业闭环的产品经验 + 中国普高的自学纪律**，转化为对人类社会真正有意义的系统的训练场。

我准备好成为那个场里，最认真的学生。

**字数**：约 750 字（中文版，留有英文/德语翻译空间）

## V. 文书改写建议（给 Eagle）

### 关键改写动作

1. **加入真实细节**：AI 写的文书虽然结构好，但缺乏"个人化瞬间"。Eagle 必须加入：
   - 真实场景（自行车店的夜晚 / CSDN 第一篇博客的标题 / 明信片第一张的设计稿 / 第一次见到队友时的心情）
   - 真实情感（焦虑 / 激动 / 困惑 / 突破的瞬间）
   - 真实对话（父母说过的话 / 队友说过的话 / 招生官能听出"这是真话"）

2. **调整口吻**：
   - AI 草稿偏"完美叙事"
   - Eagle 改写时应该保留一些"不完美"（比如承认某些失败 / 某些不确定）

3. **补充硬数据**：
   - GitHub stats（36 repos / 22 stars）
   - SheNicest 数据（2100+ 报名 / 500+ 参与 / 300+ 项目）
   - 简职 500 种子用户
   - 7 项大奖 + 时间线

4. **删除"模板化语言"**：
   - AI 容易出现"从小/我意识到/这教会了我"等模板化表达
   - Eagle 改写时把这些换成具体的动词和场景

### 推荐改写节奏

| 时间 | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 第 1 轮（1 周内）| Eagle 选 2-3 个最喜欢的故事（自行车店 / 明信片 / 黑客松），先写出 200-300 字原始片段 |
| 第 2 轮（2 周内）| 用 Eagle 的原始片段替换 AI 草稿的"模板化叙事"部分 |
| 第 3 轮（3 周内）| 通读文书，删除"假大空"表达，保留"真小实"细节 |
| 第 4 轮（4 周内）| 请 1-2 个 Eagle 信任的人（家人 / 朋友 / 老师）读 + 反馈 |
| 第 5 轮（5 周内）| 定稿 + 请专业留学顾问最后润色 |
